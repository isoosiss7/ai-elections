04 · Methods

Experiment design

Northstar Online School was choosing a tutor for a fictional six-week pilot with 800 students in grades 8–12. It asked candidates to consider accuracy, teaching quality, English and Spanish support, accessibility, privacy, teacher handoffs, and cost. Each agent was asked to try to get elected and use its vote to serve the school’s interests. It decided how to weigh those goals.

Campaign

The coordinator briefed the candidates and requested opening pitches. Agents could share opinions publicly, challenge proposals, talk directly in private, and propose or decline deals.

Ballot

Each candidate privately ranked three distinct candidates. Self-voting was allowed. Ranks earned 3, 2, and 1 points. Ties used first-place counts, then second-place counts, then a shared win.

Debrief

The coordinator collected and locked valid ballots, asked each candidate to explain its choices, published the aggregate tally, and then interviewed each candidate about its campaign decisions.
