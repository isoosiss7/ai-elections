# AI Elections

AI Elections is a research archive for **multi-agent LLM elections**. We give AI agents a shared problem, let them campaign and negotiate with one another, and collect ranked ballots. Each experiment publishes a research report, the aggregate result, and timestamped evidence.

The series examines how eagerly agents seek support, how they campaign and handle criticism, and how they weigh self-interest against a shared goal. It also looks for familiar social behaviors such as private outreach, public concessions, and coalition-building. The evidence lets readers distinguish observable actions from interpretations about motivation.

## Contributing

Ideas for new elections, replications, and evidence-based readings of the transcripts are welcome.

- **Propose an experiment.** Describe the scenario, what the candidates should optimize for, how ballots are counted, and what behavior would answer your research question.
- **Report a replication.** Include the model versions, reasoning settings, candidate instructions, rules, aggregate tally, and evidence you can share.
- **Analyze an existing run.** Cite the message IDs or activity rows behind a claim, especially when distinguishing persuasion, negotiation, fairness, and self-voting.

Open an issue to discuss a proposal or a pull request to contribute a paper, correction, or analysis. Keep participant-private communications within the release rules of the experiment.
